Diwali Mela Online — Virtual Diwali Mela 2026

Welcome to Diwali Mela Online, India's interactive 3D virtual fairground for Diwali 2026. Built to run directly in your mobile or desktop browser with no download required, this free cultural experience invites you to roam lantern-lit bazaar lanes, play classic Indian mela games, light 7 earthen diyas, paint 3D floor rangoli, burst eco-friendly patakhe, and watch 1,008 Akashdeep sky lanterns rise into the starry night sky with friends and family.

About Diwali Mela Online & Full List of 3D Mela Games

Remember childhood Diwali evenings when the entire neighborhood walked under glowing marigold torans to the town mela? Diwali Mela Online recreates that warmth and nostalgia in real-time 3D so families, hostel students, and friends living across different cities can celebrate together. Every game and ride inside the fairground is free:

  • Nishana Lagao (Balloon Shooting Stall): Aim at bobbing balloons on the wooden target board with 3 free shots per round to win the Diwali Teddy Prize.
  • Swarna Challa (Golden Ring Toss): Toss 5 golden rings onto traditional Diwali prize pedestals to win the Swarna Challa Trophy.
  • Rangoli Aangan (Interactive 3D Floor Rangoli): Dip into 8 brass bowls of vibrant gulal powder and click 25 lotus petals and star diamonds on the courtyard floor to paint a festive mandala.
  • 7 Diyas Deepotsav Mandap, Temple Bell & Maha-Aarti: Tap 7 handcrafted terracotta diyas to ignite their flames, ring the brass Mandir Ghanti, and perform a clockwise golden Maha-Aarti.
  • Maut Ka Kuan (Well of Death Stunt Game): Steer your stunt car along the vertical wooden cylinder wall and hit Turbo Boost to collect 8 golden rings in 20 seconds.
  • Patakha Zone & Wish Sky Lanterns: Sparkle a handheld Golden Phuljhari, ignite 3D Anaar fountains, spinning Chakris, and Sky Rockets, or release a floating Wish Sky Lantern carrying your prayer.
  • Giant Rides & Festive Bazaar Stalls: Ride the 28-meter Chand Wala Jhoola (Giant Ferris Wheel), Royal Horse Carousel, and Peacock Ship, and visit stalls serving Kaju Katli, Garam Jalebi, Kala Khatta Baraf Gola, and Suhaag Choodi & Mehendi.

How to Play on Mobile & Desktop

No app installation or login is needed. On mobile phones and tablets, use the bottom-left virtual thumb joystick to walk and drag anywhere on the screen to look around the 3D fairground. On laptops and desktop computers, press W, A, S, D or the Arrow keys to walk, hold Shift to run, drag with your mouse to look around, and press E to interact with shopkeepers, rides, and friendly mela visitors who greet you with “Happy Diwali!” when clicked.

How Shops & Creators Can Book a 3D Stall in the Mela

Small businesses, D2C brands, artisans, and creators can set up their own customized 3D storefront along the central bazaar lane. Click “Promote Your Shop · Setup 3D Stall” on the welcome screen or inside the top-right Diya Menu to open the 3D Stall Builder. Select an available plot on the fairground map, enter your shop name, tagline, festive canopy color palette, and interactive shopkeeper Q&A, and your 3D stall is immediately placed in the mela for visitors to discover.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is Diwali Mela Online free?

Yes! Diwali Mela Online is 100% free to play in your mobile or desktop browser with no download and no registration required.

When is Diwali 2026?

Main Diwali (Lakshmi Puja) in 2026 falls on Sunday, 8 November 2026. The 5-day festival runs from Dhanteras on Friday, 6 November 2026 to Bhai Dooj on Tuesday, 10 November 2026.

How do I play the Diwali mela games?

Click Enter Diwali Mela, walk up to any game stall using the mobile touch joystick or WASD keys (or tap a quick-teleport pill), and click or tap in 3D to shoot balloons, toss rings, paint rangoli, or steer inside Maut Ka Kuan.

Can I light a diya online for someone?

Yes, walk to the central 7 Diyas Deepotsav Mandap to light all 7 earthen clay diyas in 3D, release a personalized Wish Sky Lantern, and share a custom invitation link or 3D Postcard with your loved ones on WhatsApp.

How do I book a stall in the mela?

Open the 3D Stall Builder via the “Promote Your Shop” button, choose a bazaar plot, customize your stall name, canopy theme, and product details, and publish your 3D stall in seconds.
