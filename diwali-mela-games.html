Diwali Game Online Free — Every 3D Game, Ride & Interactive Stall in the Mela

1. Nishana Lagao — 3D Balloon Shooting Stall

No Indian Dussehra or Diwali mela is complete without the iconic air-gun balloon shooting stall! Step up to Raju Bhaiya's brightly lit wooden target board where rows of vibrant red, yellow, blue, pink, and emerald balloons gently bob in the night breeze. Each round gives you 3 free shots—simply click or tap directly on a moving balloon in 3D space to pop it with a celebratory burst of confetti and win the coveted Diwali Teddy Prize for your Mela Bag.

2. Swarna Challa — Golden Ring Toss Game

Test your timing and aim at the Swarna Challa booth! Five ornate prize pedestals—including brass lamps, handcrafted toys, and festive gift boxes—sit on the velvet table in front of you. You receive 5 golden rings per game. Click or tap any prize pedestal in 3D to launch a spinning golden ring in a parabolic arc. Land your ring cleanly around a peg to unlock the Swarna Challa Trophy.

3. Rangoli Aangan — Interactive 3D Floor Mandala Art

Right beside the central courtyard lies the illuminated Rangoli Aangan, featuring a 25-segment geometric lotus mandala carved into the fairground floor. Eight brass katoris filled with vibrant gulal powders—Sindoor Red, Marigold Gold, Rani Pink, Peacock Blue, Mango Green, Royal Purple, Haldi Yellow, and Sacred White—surround the courtyard. Click any brass bowl (or pick from the on-screen palette) and tap individual inner lotus petals, outer petals, and diamond stars on the floor to paint your own custom 3D Diwali Rangoli masterpiece.

4. Maut Ka Kuan — Well of Death Stunt Rider Game

Hear the roar of the crowd and climb the wooden ramp to the legendary Maut Ka Kuan (Well of Death)! In this 20-second high-adrenaline 3D mini-game, you pilot a stunt car racing horizontally around the vertical wooden cylinder wall. Use the Steer Higher, Steer Lower, and Turbo Boost controls (or W/S/Space keys on desktop) to intercept and collect 8 glowing golden Diwali rings suspended along the wooden walls before the timer runs out.

5. Interactive Patakha Zone & Handheld Golden Phuljhari

Enjoy the sparkle and excitement of fireworks with zero smoke or pollution! Walk to the open-air Patakha Pad near the entrance gate and click directly on the 3D Flowerpot Anaar to trigger a 5-second golden fountain of sparks, click the Ground Chakri to watch it spin rapidly across the ground in a neon circle, or ignite the Bottle Sky Rocket to launch a whistling rocket that explodes high in the starry sky. You can also toggle your handheld Golden Phuljhari sparkler anytime while roaming.

6. Chand Wala Jhoola (Giant Ferris Wheel), Royal Carousel & 1008 Akashdeep Finale

When you want to relax and soak in the view, board the 28-meter Chand Wala Jhoola (Giant Wheel), the Royal Horse Carousel, or the Mor Pankh Nauka (Swinging Peacock Ship) for a first-person panoramic ride over the illuminated fairgrounds. Finally, head north to the 20-meter Swarna Deep-Stambh tower to trigger the 1008 Akashdeep Sky Lantern & Grand Fireworks Finale.
